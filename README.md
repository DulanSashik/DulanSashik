## Hi, I'm Dulan Sashik

I'm an electronics and embedded systems engineer in Sri Lanka. I design 4-layer mixed-signal and power boards in Altium, write C/C++ firmware for STM32 and ESP32, and build Python tools for testing and data analysis. My work has mostly been for EV, battery-storage, grid-edge and seismic-sensing products.

- **Now:** R&D Engineer (Electronics) at Alta Vision.
  - REACT: controller boards and CAN charger firmware for a battery-swapping station, an Innovate UK-funded project converting tuk-tuks to electric.
  - System controllers and gateways for grid-connected batteries and rooftop solar.
- **Before:** Research Assistant at SLIIT. I designed a low-cost hybrid MEMS–geophone earthquake sensor with CRISiSLab (Massey University, NZ). It recorded a real Mw 5.4 earthquake with peak acceleration within ~5 % of a commercial reference.
- **Education:** MSc in Computer Science (in progress), University of Sri Jayewardenepura; BSc in Physical Science (Physics & Electronics), University of Kelaniya.

### Featured project

**[Peltier temperature regulator](https://github.com/DulanSashik/peltier-temperature-regulator):** a PID-controlled thermoelectric stage that heats and cools a sample, settles within ±1 °C in 24 s, and finds phase transitions from latent-heat dips. The repository includes the firmware, the raw data and the analysis.
